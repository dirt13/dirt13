Page layout for a study of Arturo Escobar's works on decolonization and decentralization from western conventions

This text is printed on a 64x960 cm poster with asymmetrical text blocks fitted together to form a map-like document,
to break away from the traditional european standards of page and text layout.
The final document is one large ensemble comprised of many seperate blocks coming together to form a message both through the text and the layout itself.

Credits: Rocio Hernandez + Justin Araya
